Persons With Disabilities

We support and empower every member.

A barangay-based association that provides assistance, livelihood programs, and caring support to persons with disabilities in Santa Cruz, Davao del Sur.

About Us

Our organization helps persons with disabilities by providing assistive devices, food packs, burial assistance, financial aid, and livelihood support. We aim to provide care, monitor members, and help them maintain a convenient and comfortable lifestyle.

Our Services

Assistive Devices

Support for mobility, health, and daily living needs.

Food & Burial Support

Emergency and essential aid for members in times of need.

Financial Assistance

Practical support to help members address urgent needs.

Livelihood Program

Programs that help members become more productive and independent.

How We Help

Members may visit the office to fill out an application form and undergo an interview. This helps identify their specific disabilities, determine eligibility, and provide the most appropriate assistance services.
